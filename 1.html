Happy Anniversary

To the one who feels like sunshine

I don't remember the exact moment it happened. Somewhere between a conversation that ran far too late and a silence that felt completely comfortable, you stopped being someone I liked and became the person I belong to.

Thank you for the patience, the terrible inside jokes, and the way you send me things that remind you of me. Thank you for making an ordinary Tuesday feel like something worth showing up for.

If I had to do all of it again, I'd still choose you — I'd just find you faster.

Us, so far —

Always yours,
